Our Delicious Story

Charkoal began its journey in 2017 with a simple purpose to create a place where people could enjoy good food beneath open skies and the gentle glow of Imphal’s evening lights. As Manipur’s first rooftop barbeque destination, it soon became a cherished spot for gatherings, celebrations, and peaceful moments shared above the city.

From the golden glow of our live rooftop barbeque to the delicate harmony in every handmade dish, each flavour carries a quiet echo of our heart. Over the years, we’ve softened our corners, refined our menu, and shaped an ambiance where every visit feels like a tender embrace warm, inviting, and touched with a little magic.

Our story is one of flavour, friendship, and quiet beauty framed by the soft, serene evenings of Imphal and we invite you to continue writing the next chapters with us.
