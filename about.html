Since 2019

The roof sat empty
for eleven years

Then somebody carried a pit up seven flights, lit it, and the whole block started asking what the smell was.

One

We cook in the open, because hiding it never made sense

Ours is the first thing you meet when the lift doors open — a live-fire grill, a long counter, ten stools, and whoever is on the pit that night working an arm's length from the people eating.

It changes how the food is cooked and how it gets served. Nobody sends out a plate they would not put in front of their own family, because they are standing right there when it lands.

Two

Everything begins with the wood

Post oak for the long cooks — it burns slow and clean and leaves the beef tasting of itself. Mesquite when we want an edge. Pecan for fish, and only for fish.

It comes from one mill outside Lockhart, from a family we have bought from since the first week, seasoned eighteen months before it reaches us. We stack it along the north wall, where you can watch the pile shrink across a Saturday night.

Three

What we will not compromise on

Bought whole

Whole briskets and whole fish, broken down here. Harder, less waste, and it is why the board changes when something runs out.

Made this morning

Bread, pickles, sauces, ice cream. If it can be made in the building before service, it is.

One seating

Your table is yours for the evening. We would rather turn people away than turn a table twice.

Everyone eats

A third of the menu is vegetarian and cooked over the same fire, not pushed to the bottom of the page.

Come and see

The best seat is at the fire counter

Ten stools, first come at the bar, or ask for it when you book.
