Our Story

The fire is the first thing you see

Most kitchens are built to be hidden. Ours is the first thing you meet when the lift doors open — a live-fire grill, a long counter, and whoever is on the pit that night working an arm's length from the people eating.

Everything here meets flame or smoke at some point. The post oak comes from one mill outside Lockhart. The bread is baked downstairs each morning. What we run out of, we run out of — and most nights, we do.
